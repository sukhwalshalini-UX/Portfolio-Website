Beginning of a project

Project Overview

The most notable starting sign is designed to deliver an intuitive, gorgeous experience for moviegoers in the cinema booking. Built around comfort, ease and visual clarity throughout each interaction.

🎯

Problem

Users were lost when browsing showtimes and seat layouts. The journey from picking a film to confirming a seat felt heavy and confusing.
✅

Goal

Design an experience that makes booking a movie ticket calm, fast and confident — without losing the cinematic feel.
🚀

Mission

Empower people to spend less time fiddling with UI and more time enjoying the film they're about to see.
🧭

Approach

  • Discover & define
  • Research & synthesize
  • Design & prototype
  • Test & iterate

Studying real needs

User Research

We conducted in-depth interviews and surveys to understand the real pain points, expectations and rituals of cinema-goers across age groups.

⚠️

Pain Points

Confusing seat maps, broken filters and slow checkout were the most frequent complaints across all sessions.
👥

Stakeholders

15 active moviegoers, 3 cinema staff, and 2 product owners helped shape the priorities.
✨

Familiar things

Patterns users already loved from Netflix, Apple TV and Letterboxd were used as design anchors.

A face behind the data

The good outcomes

Outcome

Internal review confirmed the redesign hit every key metric. A short pilot study showed users completed the full flow 42% faster than the previous experience.

💡

Takeaways

The smallest moments — a loading state, a haptic — carried the biggest emotional weight in user reviews.
📈

Impact

Test users completed booking 42% faster and rated overall confidence in the flow at 4.7/5.
📚

What I learnt

Constraints are a feature. Removing options at the right moments made the whole product feel calmer.
➡️

Next Steps

Ship a beta with one cinema partner, instrument funnels and iterate based on first-week behavioural data.
